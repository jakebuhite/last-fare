# Passenger: The Quiet One (Elias V.)

- **Status**: prototype (playable)
- **Teaches mechanic(s)**: M04 rearview mirror, M06 passenger rules, M20 ride endings
- **Shown rating / red flags**: 5.0 by 0 drivers; pickup at a cemetery closed since 9 PM; a driver note says "He never says anything."
- **Pickup > dropoff**: Mercy Hill Cemetery, north gate > 114 Aldous Street ($28)

## Behaviour
He sits in the back right and never speaks. Each time he goes unwatched for a while he is somewhere closer, with no movement in between:
0. Rear right > 1. Rear centre > 2. Behind the driver > 3. Leaning between the front seats > 4. At the driver's ear (the attack).
The unwatched time needed shrinks as he gets closer (15% less per stage).

## Hidden rules
1. He only moves when you aren't looking at him (turned around or holding the mirror).
2. A quick glance resets his timer; he doesn't need constant staring.
3. Staring for ~3 seconds pushes him back one stage.

## Pressure
Figures stand in the road at three points. Stopping for one means looking forward and not at him, and costs time while he keeps creeping.

## Endings
- **Delivered**: highest stage reached was 2 or less. He gets out. Rating +0.05, fare paid.
- **Still in the car**: he reached stage 3 at some point. The app marks the ride complete but he stays in the back seat. Rating -0.3, fare paid.
- **Attacked**: he reached stage 4. Rating -1.6, no fare (account is deactivated if rating falls below 3.5).
